1. The short version

WanderNote is a travel journal that works entirely on your device. You do not need an account to use it. If you choose to sign in, an account is used only to sync your trips between your own devices.

2. Information we collect

  • Trips, notes and packing lists — stored on your device. Synced to our server only if you sign in.
  • Account information — only if you create an account: your email address and a password managed by our authentication provider. We never see your password.
  • Photos — photo files stay on your device. Only a local reference to each photo is stored, so your images are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Location — if you grant permission, your coordinates are used on the device to calculate how far you are from a destination. This is computed locally and is never transmitted or stored.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or contact lists. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Third-party services

WanderNote sends data to the following services, and only for the purposes described:

  • Google Gemini (via our Cloudflare Workers proxy) — powers AI travel writing. When you tap Generate, the trip content you selected is sent to produce that text. It is not used to train models, and we do not retain it.
  • Supabase (servers in the United States) — account sign-in and optional cloud sync of trips and lists. Used only if you sign in.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts. Receives destination coordinates only. No account or API key involved.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — converts a place name into coordinates. Receives the place name only.
  • Apple Maps — renders the footprint map on your device.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Storage and security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Data stays on your device unless you sign in and enable sync.
  • Synced data is stored on Supabase servers and is readable only by your account.

5. Your choices

  • Use the app without an account, keeping everything on your device.
  • Delete any trip, day, note or list inside the app at any time.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Delete Account. This removes your synced data and your account.
  • Revoke photo, camera or location permission at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Children

WanderNote is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the date above.

1. 一句话说明

WanderNote 是一款完全在你手机本地运行的旅行日记应用。不需要注册账号就能完整使用。如果你选择登录，账号仅用于在你自己的多台设备之间同步旅程。

2. 我们收集的信息

  • 旅程、随记和打包清单 —— 保存在你的设备上。只有在你登录后才会同步到服务器。
  • 账号信息 —— 仅在你主动注册时：邮箱地址，以及由认证服务托管的密码。我们看不到你的密码。
  • 照片 —— 照片文件始终留在你的设备上。我们只保存指向照片的本地引用，图片本身不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 位置 —— 在你授权的前提下，坐标仅在设备本地用于计算你与目的地的距离。该计算在本机完成，不会传输，也不会存储。

我们不收集统计分析数据、广告标识符或通讯录，也不会跨其他应用或网站追踪你。

3. 第三方服务

WanderNote 会向以下服务发送数据，且仅限于所述用途：

  • Google Gemini（经由我们的 Cloudflare Workers 代理）—— 提供 AI 旅行写作功能。你点击「生成」时，才会把你选中的旅程内容发送过去用于生成文本。这些内容不用于训练模型，我们也不留存。
  • Supabase（服务器位于美国）—— 账号登录，以及可选的旅程与清单云同步。仅在你登录后使用。
  • Open-Meteo —— 天气预报。仅接收目的地坐标，无需账号或 API 密钥。
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim —— 将地点名称转换为坐标。仅接收地点名称。
  • Apple 地图 —— 在你的设备上绘制足迹地图。

我们不会将你的个人信息出售给任何人。

4. 存储与安全

  • 所有网络传输均使用 HTTPS 加密。
  • 除非你登录并启用同步，数据始终留在你的设备上。
  • 已同步的数据存放在 Supabase 服务器，只有你的账号可以读取。

5. 你的选择

  • 不注册账号直接使用，所有内容都留在本机。
  • 随时在应用内删除任意旅程、某一天、随记或清单。
  • 在「我的 → 注销账号」删除账号，这会一并删除你已同步的数据和账号本身。
  • 随时在 iOS 系统设置中撤销照片、相机或位置权限。

6. 儿童隐私

WanderNote 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的个人信息。
