本地版隐私说明 · Local Edition Privacy Policy · 2026-10-20

1. 本机记录 / On-device records

本地版无需账号。旅程、日期、笔记、清单、所选照片、昵称和头像保存在设备的 App 存储空间，不会由本版本上传到 WanderNote 的账号或云同步服务。旅行草稿和行李模板在本机整理，不向在线 AI 服务发送笔记。

This edition requires no account. Trips, notes, checklists, selected media and profile preferences are stored in the app on your device. This edition does not upload them to a WanderNote account or cloud sync service. Drafts and packing templates are prepared locally, without sending notes to an online AI service.

2. 没有备份 / No in-app backup

本版本不提供云同步、备份、导出或导入功能。卸载 App、更换手机或设备损坏可能导致记录无法恢复。不要将本 App 作为重要资料的唯一保存位置。

This version has no in-app cloud sync, backup, export or import. Uninstalling the app, replacing a device or device damage may make records unrecoverable. Do not keep your only copy of important material here. Device-level backups, if any, are controlled by your operating system and settings; WanderNote does not provide or guarantee recovery through them.

3. 网络服务 / Network services

本地存储不代表所有功能都离线。城市搜索和地理编码向 OpenStreetMap Nominatim 发送您输入的城市或搜索文字；天气向 Open-Meteo 发送查询地点的坐标和时区。地图与停车导航使用 Apple 地图服务，可能传输地图区域或导航坐标。这些服务还会收到网络请求通常包含的连接信息（例如 IP 地址），并适用各自的隐私政策。断网时天气、搜索和地图可能不可用，已保存在本机的记录仍可访问。

Local storage does not mean every feature is offline. City search and geocoding send the entered search text to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Weather requests send the requested coordinates and time zone to Open-Meteo. Maps and parking navigation use Apple Maps services and may send map regions or navigation coordinates. Providers receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, under their own privacy policies. These network features may be unavailable offline; saved local records remain accessible.

4. 权限与 Apple Watch / Permissions and Apple Watch

相机和照片权限用于您主动选择或拍摄照片、编辑并保存图片。定位权限用于距离、海拔、日落计算及您主动保存的停车位置。Apple Watch 可通过 WatchConnectivity 接收并缓存 iPhone 的旅行概览；手表也会在获得相应权限后读取本地定位与步数。拒绝权限时，相应数据可能显示不可用；权限可在系统设置中管理。

Camera and photo access support your chosen media and image edits. Location supports distances, altitude, sun calculations and parking locations you choose to save. Apple Watch receives and caches travel snapshots from your paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity, and reads location and steps with permission. Denied permissions make related information unavailable and can be managed in system settings.

5. 删除与分享 / Deletion and sharing

您可在 App 中删除旅程、笔记或清单。卸载 App 会删除其本机存储；已另存到系统照片库、Apple 健康或其他接收方的数据，以及手表上的缓存，不会因此自动从那些位置删除。您主动复制、分享或发送邮件时，所选内容会交给您选择的系统功能或接收方。本版本没有广告 SDK、广告跟踪、订阅或应用内购买。

You can delete trips, notes and lists in the app. Uninstalling removes its local storage, but does not automatically remove copies in Photos, Apple Health, recipients’ apps or Watch caches. Copying, sharing or emailing content is initiated by you and passes that content to your chosen system feature or recipient. This edition includes no advertising SDK, advertising tracking, subscriptions or In-App Purchases.

6. 历史云数据与联系 / Previous cloud data and contact

本地版不会自动下载、迁移或删除旧版账号的云端数据。如您使用过旧版云端账号，并希望查询或删除历史云数据，请联系下方邮箱。发送支持邮件时，我们会收到您自愿提供的邮件内容。后续版本如改变数据处理方式，会更新此说明。

This edition does not automatically download, migrate or delete data from accounts used in earlier cloud-enabled versions. For questions or deletion requests concerning those historical records, contact us below. Support emails contain only the information you choose to send. This notice will be updated if a later edition changes these practices.
