Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

WanderNote is a travel memory and planning app. We respect user privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to provide the app experience.

Information We May Collect

WanderNote may store trip information, travel notes, packing lists, destination data, photos selected by the user, and account information when sign-in or cloud sync is used.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide travel records, maps, packing lists, AI-assisted travel content, and account-related features.

Third-Party Services

WanderNote may use third-party services for authentication, cloud storage, AI generation, maps, weather, and app distribution.

User Control

Users may delete travel records inside the app. For account or data deletion requests, contact us by email.
