# WanderNote 1.1.2 App Store Launch Plan

This plan is aligned with the current local-first travel journal. It does not promise online AI,
cloud sync, backup, import/export, advertising, or paid features.

## Positioning

Primary message: keep trip plans and memories organized on your device.

Lead with trips, daily notes/photos, reusable packing checklists, local writing templates, and
clear local storage. Treat Apple Watch as a supporting companion, not the lead promise, until
paired physical-device verification and current Watch screenshots are complete.

## Release Materials

- Metadata and review notes: [1.1.2 release pack](app-store-1.1.2-release-pack.md).
- Privacy policy: `privacy-policy.html`; publish it to the existing public URL and verify it
  loads before submitting.
- iPhone screenshots: capture from the final submitted build with representative, non-personal
  sample data. Required current target: iPhone 17 Pro, 1206 × 2622 portrait.
- Apple Watch screenshots: required for the included Watch app; use current physical Watch UI
  at one consistent size. Do not claim sensor behavior that has not been tested.
- App icons: iPhone and Watch asset catalogs should show the same WanderNote artwork.

## After Release

Review App Store Connect analytics weekly for impressions, product page views, first-time
downloads, and conversion rate. Low impressions point to discovery/keyword work; page views
without downloads point to positioning/screenshots. Avoid adding features until there is evidence
from user feedback or funnel data.

Do not begin a product page optimization test or custom product pages until the standard page is
complete and receives enough traffic to compare variants meaningfully.
