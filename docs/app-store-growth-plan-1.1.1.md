# WanderNote 1.1.1 App Store Growth Plan

Date: 2026-10-14
Scope: App Store product-page and conversion work for WanderNote 1.1.1.

This plan is intentionally small. The goal is to improve App Store discovery and conversion before adding more app features.

## Current Positioning

WanderNote is a travel journal with practical trip tools and an Apple Watch companion.

Primary promise:

> Plan, remember, and glance at your trips from iPhone and Apple Watch.

What is newly marketable in 1.1.1:

- Apple Watch companion for trip glance.
- Travel Mode with destination, weather, updated time, stale/cache handling.
- Daily Glance with altitude, sun, steps, and parking fallback.
- Return to Parking on Apple Watch.
- Travel Story / AI creation with local fallback when online AI is unavailable.

Do not lead with:

- Subscription, Pro, paywall, or premium claims.
- Complex AI claims that imply guaranteed online generation.
- Standalone Watch app language until real-device verification is fully passed.

## Immediate Goal

Improve product-page conversion, not feature count.

Measure:

- Product page impressions.
- Product page views.
- First-time downloads.
- Conversion rate.
- Source split: App Store search, browse, web referrer, custom product pages.

Interpretation:

- Low impressions means discovery/keyword problem.
- Good impressions but low downloads means screenshots/title/subtitle problem.
- Downloads but low usage means onboarding/core value problem.

## Product Page Optimization Test

Apple supports product page optimization for iOS/iPadOS product pages with up to three treatments. It is not available for Apple Watch-only product pages or custom product pages.

Run one simple test:

- Baseline: current product page.
- Treatment A: iPhone travel journal lead.
- Treatment B: Apple Watch companion lead.
- Treatment C: AI travel story lead.

Do not test more than one big idea per treatment. Keep title/subtitle fixed if possible and vary screenshot order/creative first.

Success threshold:

- Wait until each treatment has enough impressions and at least several first-time downloads.
- Prefer a treatment only when conversion improvement is visible and not just a one-day spike.

## Custom Product Pages

Apple allows custom product pages to highlight specific features or content, and each page can have its own screenshots, previews, promotional text, and keywords. Use them for external links or search-keyword routing after approval.

Create only two at first:

1. Watch Companion page
   - Audience: Apple Watch users, travel/outdoor users.
   - First screenshot: Watch Travel Mode.
   - Keywords to consider assigning after approval: watch, apple watch, travel watch, trip glance.

2. Travel Journal page
   - Audience: people searching for diary/trip-memory tools.
   - First screenshot: trip detail or story.
   - Keywords to consider assigning after approval: travel journal, travel diary, trip planner, packing list.

Do not create many custom pages until there is enough traffic to compare them.
